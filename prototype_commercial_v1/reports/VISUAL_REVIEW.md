# P1-C-A1 visual reality review

## Geometry authority

All delivered PNGs are tessellated from the CadQuery B-Rep used to export `step/P1C_ASSEMBLY.step`. No image-generation model, painted-in fastener, or invented body panel is used. The neutral CAD views are intentionally less photorealistic than concept art, but preserve exact panel thicknesses, fastener positions, seams, spring position, mounting slots, and proportions.

## What is structural

- P1C-S001 folded 3 mm steel tray.
- P1C-S002 and P1C-S003 formed 3 mm steel side frames.
- P1C-S004 5 mm steel lever.
- P1C-S005 spring bracket and P1C-S006 removable clamp bracket.
- M8 pivot, dual 608 bearings, M6 steel anchors/stops, and standard hardware.

## What is cosmetic or non-structural

- P1C-A001/A002 2 mm brushed-aluminium accents.
- Aluminium grip trim rings and the short dark-red printed spring collar.
- Printed electronics lid and covers. The electronics pod protects components but is not in the primary hand-load path.

## Accent attachment

Each aluminium accent is mounted proud of its steel frame on 2 mm spacers using three M4 black button-head screws. The installation view displaces the accents and screws only along their real assembly axes. The accents do not carry pivot or spring load.

The right accent is a continuous one-piece aluminium panel. It cosmetically covers the Hall zone; the required capsule window is in the ferromagnetic steel frame behind it. This keeps the visible accent manufacturable and the local sensing layer non-ferromagnetic.

## Customer-visible mechanism

The customer sees the black steel frame, brushed aluminium accents, M8 pivot trim, three lever apertures, commercial-grip envelope, black extension spring, short red guide collar, rear pod, base slots, and consistent black fasteners. The hub, Hall sled, magnet carrier, Hall board, cable guide, stop contact geometry, and controller remain screened or internal.

## Weaknesses found and disposition

1. Early Hall close-ups hid the air gap in projection. The final view is aligned with the X axis and shows the magnet/board separation without changing CAD.
2. Early context framing made the product too small. The final context uses translucent generic unbranded fixtures and focuses on the exact assembly.
3. The concept-art expectation exceeds the available local renderer. The delivered images are exact-CAD material renders rather than generative photorealism; this avoids geometry hallucination.
4. The strict full-height rear view necessarily makes the 54 × 64 × 30 mm pod visually small relative to the 300 mm lever. The orthographic rear image remains dimensionally honest.

## Physical reality still requiring validation

Powder-coat texture, aluminium brushing, panel-gap appearance after bending, purchased-grip surface, spring appearance, fastener coatings, formed-part alignment, and magnetic response near the steel chassis must be checked on a fabricated alpha.
