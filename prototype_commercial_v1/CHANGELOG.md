- Replaced printed chassis with folded steel tray and paired formed side frames.
- Added non-structural brushed-aluminium accents and original tapered lever.
- Integrated protected magnetic window, Hall sled, rear electronics pod, removable steel clamp and rig slots.
- Added manufacturing DXFs, motion/clamp states, mass/load/magnetic calculations, RFQ pack and cost model.

- Reduced the red part to a short spring guide collar and cleared the Hall sled from the complete magnet arc.
- Kept the right aluminium accent continuous while retaining the non-ferromagnetic steel-frame Hall window; both accents now export as one-piece laser-cut solids.
- Reworked the hidden cable guide as a support-free U-channel with a separate M3 mounting floor; all ten released STLs are watertight after standard vertex welding.
- Generated 18 exact-CAD customer and engineering views plus a render manifest; no generative geometry was used.
