# P1.0 prototype BOM

Quantities are for one handbrake unless marked spare. Lengths are purchase candidates and must be checked against actual washers/nuts and local stock.

## Printed PETG

Nineteen structural/accessory prints: P1-001, P1-002, P1-003, P1-004, P1-006 through P1-013 (including P1-011A/B), and P1-020 through P1-025. Print the eight calibration coupons first. Allow approximately 1.5–2.0 kg PETG for coupons, iterations, and all parts; slicer mass depends on settings.

## M6 structural hardware

Buy a zinc-plated assortment of M6x20/25/30/35/40/45, large and standard washers, and at least 20 nyloc nuts. Initial allocation:

- 4 x M6x35 tower-to-base through-bolts.
- 2 x M6x40 lever-to-hub bolts.
- 2 x M6x25 spring-module base bolts.
- 2 x M6x30 spring anchor pins/bolts, one fixed and one moving.
- 1 x M6x45 moving travel-stop pin.
- 2 x M6x25 stop-adjust screws with rubber caps/bumpers and locknuts.
- 4 x M6x30 clamp-interface through-bolts.
- 4 x M6x30 clamp butt-joint through-bolts.
- 2 x M6 rig bolts of length appropriate to the rig plus washers at least 18 mm OD.

## M4/M3 accessory hardware

- 4 x M4x35 plus washers/nylocs for the two stop blocks.
- 2 x M4x30 plus washers/nylocs for Hall fixed bracket.
- 4 x M4x20 plus washers/nylocs for electronics enclosure base.
- 1 x M4x40 plus nyloc for grip retention.
- 4 x M3x20 plus nuts for Hall sled clamping.
- 2 x M3x12 and 1 x M3x20 for magnet holder/keeper.
- 4 x M3x10 and four light-duty heat-set inserts for electronics cover only.
- 2 small M2.5/M3 board screws after the Hall PCB is measured.

## Spring

- Extension-spring assortment: 15–20 mm OD, 60–100 mm hook-to-hook, several light/medium rates.
- Do not buy a costly precision spring yet. P1 hook distances are 69.76–85.82 mm.

## Clamp

- 1 x M10x100 steel screw/bolt or threaded rod with a secure knob attachment.
- 1 x M10 steel coupling nut, nominal 30 mm long, about 17 mm AF; measure actual hardware.
- 1 x common M10 swivel levelling foot or swivel-pad hardware compatible with the 46 mm printed carrier.
- 1 x 46 mm rubber lower pad/disc.
- 1 x approximately 90 x 62 x 2–3 mm upper rubber/neoprene pad.
- M6 clamp fasteners listed above.

## Sensor/electronics

- 1 x SS49E/49E-family linear Hall sensor or small breakout board.
- 1 x 10 x 5 x 3 mm neodymium block magnet candidate; optional 6 x 3 mm disc for comparison.
- 1 x Arduino Pro Micro / ATmega32U4.
- 1 x JST-XH or similar 3-pin connector pair and flexible 3-wire cable.
- USB cable compatible with the selected Pro Micro.
- Heat-shrink, cable tie/strain-relief material, and optional calibration pushbutton/LED for later bench work.

## Tools/consumables

6.6 mm, 4.5 mm and pilot drills; 8 mm reamer or suitable drill; files/deburring tool; hacksaw; square; caliper; thread-locker for metal fasteners where appropriate; luggage scale/force gauge; PETG; rubber-safe adhesive. Adhesive is not a primary structural fastener.
